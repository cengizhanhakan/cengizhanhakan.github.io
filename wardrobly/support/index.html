Wardrobly Privacy Terms

Wardrobly Support

Email admin@investbud.de. Include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the Wardrobly version from Settings.

Account

Signing in

You can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and a password.

Deleting my account

Open Settings inside Wardrobly and delete your account there. It permanently removes the account and every image stored for it. You can also ask for deletion by emailing admin@investbud.de.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel

The subscription is billed by Apple, so it is managed in your Apple Account, not in the app. Open Apple Account subscriptions, or go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Turn auto-renew off at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I already paid but the features are locked

Open Settings inside Wardrobly and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. If it still does not unlock, email me with the date of purchase.

Refunds

Refunds are issued by Apple, not by me. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Wardrobe

An item got the wrong tags

The category, colour and style tags come from a clothing classification model and can be edited on the item. The paid smart retag feature can also re-read a photo and propose new attributes.

What happens to my photos

They are stored in a private bucket and are only reachable through links that expire after one hour. EXIF and GPS metadata are stripped on upload. See the privacy policy.

Feed

Reporting a post or a person

Every post, comment and profile can be reported from inside the app, and you can block an account so its content no longer reaches you. Reports are reviewed and acted on. Harassment and objectionable content are not tolerated — see the Terms of Use.

What other people can see

Your display name, avatar, bio and anything you post to the feed. If you connect with a partner, that partner also sees your email address.

Bugs and feature requests

Both go to admin@investbud.de. For a bug, describe what you did and what happened instead; a screen recording helps.
