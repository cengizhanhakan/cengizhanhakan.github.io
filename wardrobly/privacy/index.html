Wardrobly Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026.

Wardrobly is an iPhone app published by Cengizhan Hakan, Berlin, Germany, who is responsible for the processing described here (the controller). Questions about this policy, and any request about your data, go to admin@investbud.de.

What is collected

Account

Your email address, display name, avatar, bio, locale and timezone. You sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and a password.

Photos

The clothing and avatar images you upload. EXIF and GPS metadata are stripped on upload, so the location and the camera details a photo carries are removed before it is stored.

For virtual try-on, a full-body photo of yourself and the outfit images rendered from it. You can remove the try-on photo in the app at any time.

Garment attributes

A clothing classification model reads each item photo and produces attributes: category, colour family, style, season and occasion tags, formality, and a confidence value. It reads garments only. There is no face detection, body detection or pose detection anywhere in the app.

Text you write

Item titles, your profile bio, post captions, comments, feedback messages, abuse reports and partner notes.

Social graph

Who you follow, and what you like, save and block.

Device identifiers

If you allow notifications, the push token of your device, with its platform and language, so the app can tell you when a try-on is ready or someone interacts with you. It is removed when you turn notifications off, sign out, or the device stops accepting them.

A DeviceCheck token from Apple. It lets Wardrobly read and set two flags Apple keeps for your device, recording whether it has already received the starter credits and a referral reward, so deleting and recreating an account does not grant them again. The token itself is not stored.

Subscription status

Whether this account is entitled to the paid features, held by RevenueCat. Apple handles the payment; Wardrobly never sees your card, your Apple Account or your billing address.

Product analytics

A fixed set of named events, sent to PostHog's EU region. Autocapture and session recording are off. No photo, no image link and nothing you have written is included in an analytics event.

Analytics is off until you turn it on, during setup or in Settings. While it is on, the app also sends crash reports to PostHog: the error type and stack trace, the app version, and the device model and iOS version, linked to your account ID. A crash report contains no photo and nothing you have written.

What is not collected

No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because nothing here is used for advertising. No location, no contacts, no microphone access, no device fingerprinting. Your data is never sold.

What other people see

Your display name, your avatar, your bio and anything you post to the feed are visible to other users.

If you connect with a partner, that partner also sees your email address.

Where it is stored

The servers are at Hetzner in Germany. Images are stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket; the bucket is not public, and an image is served only through a signed link that expires after one hour.

OpenRouter

The AI style coach sends garment metadata only — the attributes and tags listed above. It never sends an image.

The paid smart retag feature is the one exception: it sends a temporary link to a single garment photo to a vision model so the model can re-read that item's attributes.

fal.ai

When you start a virtual try-on, fal.ai receives temporary links to your try-on photo and to the images of the garments in the outfit, and returns the rendered image. The links expire after one hour. Nothing else about you or your account is sent.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app, from Settings. Deletion is permanent and removes the account along with every image stored for it. You can also request deletion by emailing admin@investbud.de.

Children

Wardrobly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new effective date.
