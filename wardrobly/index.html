Wardrobly

Photograph your clothes, keep them in one catalogue, and get outfit suggestions.

Take a photo of an item and Wardrobly adds it to your wardrobe with tags for category, colour, style, season and occasion. From there it suggests outfits from what you already own.

There is also a feed: post an outfit, follow other people, like and save what they share.

Download on the App Store

Subscription

Wardrobly has a paid tier sold as an auto-renewable subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple Account, and it renews until you turn auto-renew off in your Apple Account settings. See the Terms of Use.

Photos

Images are stored in a private bucket and reached only through links that expire after one hour. EXIF and GPS metadata are removed when a photo is uploaded. The app does no face, body or pose detection.
